# Contributing to Wardwatch

Thanks for helping. Wardwatch is open source and public from day one.

## The CLA, and why we ask for one

Before we can merge your first pull request, you sign our [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). You keep the copyright in your work. The CLA gives the Wardwatch maintainer a broad license to it.

We ask for this for two reasons:

1. **App Store distribution.** Apple's App Store terms conflict with the GPL, and only copyright holders can object to that. Holding a license from every contributor lets us ship the GPL app through the App Store while keeping the source open for everyone else.
2. **Relicensing later.** If the project ever needs a different license, the CLA makes that possible without tracking down every past contributor.

### How to sign

When you open your first pull request, the CLA bot posts a comment. Reply with the exact sentence it gives you:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

You only sign once. The bot records your signature on the `cla-signatures` branch of this repository. If you are contributing for your employer, make sure you are allowed to sign, as described in section 5 of the CLA.

## Pull requests

- Keep each PR to one change, with a clear description.
- Every PR runs the checks in `.github/workflows/`. They must pass before merge.
- Report security problems privately to security@wardwatch.ai, not in a public issue.
